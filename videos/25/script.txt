Have you ever wondered why some soils struggle to grow healthy plants, even when they receive enough water and fertilizer?
One substance that can help improve soil conditions is humic acid.
Humic acid is an organic substance formed through the long-term decomposition of plant and animal materials in the soil.
It plays an important role in improving soil structure, helping the soil retain moisture, and making certain nutrients more available to plants.
It can also support root development and increase beneficial biological activity in the soil.
Humic acid is commonly available in three forms: liquid, powder, and granules.
Each form has its own application method, so the right amount depends on the product concentration, plant type, and soil conditions.
But remember, humic acid is not a replacement for NPK fertilizers.
It works alongside them to create better conditions for plant growth.
So, healthy plants start with healthy soil!
