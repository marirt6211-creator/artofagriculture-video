# Description
Did you know a gentle breeze can help plants grow stronger? 🌬️

When plants sway in the wind, they sense mechanical stimulation and respond with Thigmomorphogenesis: shorter, thicker stems, stronger supporting tissues and adaptive roots. It's nature's gentle workout! 💪🌿
But too much wind can damage plants, so balance is key.
