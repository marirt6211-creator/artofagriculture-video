## Description
Beneath your feet lies a living universe. Billions of microbes, fungal networks and earthworms keep soil alive — and soil keeps us alive.

In this video you'll discover:
• What soil is really made of (minerals, organic matter, water, air and life)
• How bacteria recycle nutrients
• Mycorrhizae: the secret partnership between fungi and plant roots
• How water moves through soil into roots and up into every leaf
• Why erosion, chemicals and unsustainable farming threaten this fragile world
