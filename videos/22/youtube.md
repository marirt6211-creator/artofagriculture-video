# Title
What Do NPK Numbers Mean? 20-20-20 vs 10-52-10 Explained 🌱 #shorts

# Description
Ever wondered what the numbers on fertilizer bags mean? 🤔 20-20-20, 30-10-10, 10-52-10, 12-12-36…

They show how much of the three key nutrients a fertilizer contains: Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P) and Potassium (K).

🟢 Nitrogen: leaves, stems and green, healthy growth (chlorophyll)
🟠 Phosphorus: energy, root development, flowering and seeds
🟣 Potassium: water regulation, sugar transport and stress tolerance

📊 Common NPK fertilizers:
• 20-20-20: balanced, for general feeding
• 30-10-10: high nitrogen, for leafy green growth
• 10-52-10: high phosphorus, for early growth and roots
• 12-12-36: high potassium, for fruit quality and filling

⚠️ More fertilizer doesn't always mean better growth! Overuse can upset nutrient balance, damage roots and reduce crop quality. Know what your plant actually needs before you choose.
